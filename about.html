About Dr. Gloria N. Raphael JP, HRM, CSP/SPY

Human Resource Professional | Consultant | Trainer

Dr. Gloria N. Raphael is an accomplished Human Resource Professional, Consultant, Trainer, and Strategic HR Partner passionate about people development, organizational growth, leadership development, and building effective workplace systems.

Professional Background

Dr. Gloria N. Raphael is a seasoned Human Resource Management professional with over 20 years of total work experience across diverse sectors, including manufacturing, consulting, media, information technology, real estate, and recycling industries.

She has held strategic leadership positions where she contributed to human resource management, people development, organizational effectiveness, and business growth. Her career journey reflects extensive experience in building teams, improving workplace systems, and aligning people strategies with organizational goals.

Academic Qualifications

Dr. Gloria N. Raphael holds a PhD in Human Resource Management from Atlantic International University, USA. She also holds a Master's Degree in Economics from the University of Lagos, Akoka, Lagos State.

She obtained her Bachelor's Degree in Economics from the University of Ibadan, Oyo State, and her Bachelor's Degree in Veterinary Science/Medicine from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka.

With extensive experience in Human Resource Management, recruitment, employee development, performance management, HR policies, and organizational development, Dr. Gloria helps individuals and organizations achieve excellence through strategic people management solutions.
